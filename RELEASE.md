**Lead Sniper 3 finds every request for your services in your Facebook groups — 20 local services, from moving and cleaning to plumbing and snow removal — in English and Québec French, and has a personal reply ready in one click.** With Pro, it replies for you.

### Install (2 minutes)
1. Download **lead-sniper.zip** below and double-click it to unzip. Keep the folder somewhere permanent, like Documents.
2. Open `brave://extensions` (or `chrome://extensions`), turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked** and pick the folder.
3. On the welcome screen, enter your business name and your group links, then scroll Facebook.

### What's inside
- 🧰 **New in 3.0: 20 services.** Pick yours on the Services page; each trade ignores its own ads, job posts and things for sale

- 🧹 Skips competitors' ads, job offers, sale posts and testimonials
- ✍️ Smart replies written from each customer's own words, in their language
- 🧭 Group Hunter: finds and ranks local groups by requests for your services

- 🤖 **Pro Autopilot**: replies post themselves, with limits, quiet hours and a free Rehearsal mode
