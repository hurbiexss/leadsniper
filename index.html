Is it safe for my Facebook account?

It reads pages like you do, at a human pace, and never asks for your password. Autopilot posts from your account and Facebook can limit accounts that post automatically — so Pro has limits, quiet hours and a free Rehearsal mode.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Leads, replies and settings stay in your browser on your computer.

What happens when Pro ends?

Autopilot pauses with a “Renew” button — everything else keeps working. You get a reminder 3 days before.
