Questions

Good questions.

Is it safe for my Facebook account?

Lead Sniper reads pages the way you do, in your own logged-in browser, at a human pace — it never asks for your password. Finding leads and one-click replies are just you, faster. Autopilot posts from your account, and Facebook can limit accounts that post automatically, so Pro comes with hourly and daily limits, quiet hours, a wait between replies and a free Rehearsal mode. You decide how far to go.

Does it really understand Québec French?

Yes — it was built in Gatineau on real local group posts. It reads joual, franglais, missing accents and typos (“qqn aurait un truck”), and answers French posts in French.

Where do my leads go? Is there a cloud?

There's no cloud. Everything — posts, leads, replies, settings — stays in your browser on your computer. No account, no server, no AI service. Back it up to a file whenever you like.

Is it only for movers?

It's tuned for moving, pickup & delivery, heavy items (pianos, safes, appliances) and junk removal. Turn each one on or off, add your own phrases and service areas.

What happens when Pro ends?

Autopilot pauses with a “Renew” button and you get a reminder three days before. Everything else keeps working, and your leads and settings stay. Renew early and the new days are added to the ones you have left.

Can I try Pro first?

Yes. Rehearsal mode is free forever and shows exactly what Autopilot would post. Want the real thing? Ask for a free 7-day trial key.

Why Bitcoin or Monero?

Fast, private and the same from anywhere. Tell us your browser code and plan; we reply with the exact amount and an address, and your key arrives once the payment confirms.

Which browsers and computers?

Brave and Google Chrome on Mac, Windows or Linux, with Facebook in the browser. Phones aren't supported.
